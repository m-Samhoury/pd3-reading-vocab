## Thresholds

- **Tier 1** – every connector found in the in-scope exams, plus connectors named in the Sokolova guide (`source: guide`).
- **Tier 2** – L3 option in ≥1 exam, OR question-side occurrence (L1/L2A question, L2A option, L2B sentence, L3 gap sentence) in ≥2 exams. Ranked by a weighted score: L3 option ×4, L3 gap sentence ×3, L2A/L2B option ×2, L2A question ×2, L1 question ×1.5.
- **Tier 3** – reading-text occurrence in ≥2 exams AND ≥3 occurrences in total. Ranked by number of exams, then total occurrences.
- Excluded: A1–A2 basic vocabulary, proper nouns, numbers; a word belongs only to the highest tier it qualifies for.
