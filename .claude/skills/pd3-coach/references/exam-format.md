# PD3 exam format and grading

Sources: Elena Sokolova, "How to prepare for Prøve i Dansk 3" (Feb 2026), and the official
Censor- og eksaminatorhæfter in this collection. Level is B2 (CEFR, "Vantage").

## Written exam (skriftlig del)

| Part | Task | Time | Points | Dictionary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Læseforståelse 1 | 15 short answers about a brochure/guide-style text (often a town, an education, a service) | 25 min, sheet collected after | 15 × 1 | No |
| Læseforståelse 2A | 1 text, 3 multiple-choice questions (A/B/C) | 65 min shared with 2B | 3 × 2 = 6 | No |
| Læseforståelse 2B | 1 text with 5 gaps; insert 5 of 7 given text pieces (A–G, 2 don't fit) | (shared) | 5 × 2 = 10 | No |
| Læseforståelse 3 | 1 text with 8 gaps; choose 1 of 4 words/phrases (A–D) per gap | on the back of the 2A/2B sheet | 8 × 1 = 8 | No |
| Skriftlig fremstilling | Delprøve 1: reply to an email (compulsory). Delprøve 2: choose 2A **or** 2B, argumentative text, minimum 200 words | 2½ hours total, you manage the split | one overall grade | All paper dictionaries |

Reading total: **39 points**, converted to one grade with that sitting's omregningstabel.
Example (May–June 2024): 38–39 → 12, 33–37 → 10, 27–32 → 7, 22–26 → 4, 20–21 → 02, 10–19 → 00, 0–9 → −3.
So ~20/39 passes reading; ~27/39 gives 7. The table moves a little each sitting.

Format history (matters when using old papers):
- **From Nov 2022 (2022v)**: L1, L2A (3 MC), L2B (5 text pieces), L3 (8 gaps).
- **Before Nov 2022**: L1 the same; old 2A = 7 MC questions over 2 texts (same skill as today's 2A, just longer);
  old 2B = 8 gaps with 4 options (= today's L3). Today's 2B (insert text pieces) only exists from 2022v.
- Sokolova recommends papers from ~2012 onwards for format familiarity.

### Writing — what the censors look for (Bedømmelsesvejledning, Aug 2024)
- Only Delprøve 1 answered → max grade 00 (fail). Only Delprøve 2 answered → automatically one grade lower.
- **Delprøve 2 decides the grade**, especially its last bullet (the vurdering/assessment), which must be ~50% of the answer — that's where B2 is tested.
- A top grade needs **complexity**: a complete, coherent answer in simple correct language rarely gets a top grade.

Delprøve 2A pattern: a chart/statistic → (1) beskriv kort hovedtrækkene, (2) fortæl om årsager til forskellene, (3) vurdér … begrund dine synspunkter (~50%).
Delprøve 2B pattern: a topic with a list of opinions → (1) fortæl kort om …, (2) kommenter én eller to af holdningerne, (3) vurdér fordele og ulemper … begrund (~50%).
Delprøve 1 pattern: a mail from a Danish friend with 3 underlined parts → thank, answer each underlined part, plus one extra instruction (e.g. propose a phone call).

## Oral exam (mundtlig kommunikation)

| Part | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Delprøve 1 | Present a topic you got by mail ~7 working days before (≈2 min alone), then follow-up questions (≈2½ min) | 5 min incl. instructions |
| Delprøve 2 | Unprepared topic (a picture/sheet is only inspiration — don't describe it). Mandatory + follow-up questions | ≈4½ min (5 incl. instructions) |

Examiner question types (from the official instructions, both parts):
1. **Opklarende** – "Du sagde, at … Vil du godt forklare det lidt nærmere?"
2. **Uddybende** – "Kan du ikke fortælle lidt mere om det?"
3. **Forklaring og begrundelse** – "…men hvorfor tror du så, at …?"
4. **Stillingtagen og argumentation** – "Mener du, at …? Hvorfor/hvorfor ikke?"

Delprøve 2 counts more (unprepared language). A great presentation cannot rescue a weak Delprøve 2.

## Final result

Three grades on the 7-trins-skala (12, 10, 7, 4, 02, 00, −3): Reading, Writing, Oral.
**Final = (Reading + Writing + 2 × Oral) / 4.** Oral counts double. 00 and −3 are failing grades;
Sokolova's guide: an overall average above 02 is a pass.

Topic areas that recur: Danmark før og nu, arbejde, uddannelse, medborgerskab og hverdagsliv, miljø og sundhed.
