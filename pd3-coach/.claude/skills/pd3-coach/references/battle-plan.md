# The learner's own Battle Plan (reading + writing, v2)

Transcribed from the learner's handwritten "Battle Plan_reading+writing_v2.pdf". This is **their** plan:
coach to it, quote it back, and hold them to it. Spelling slips in the original are corrected here and
marked † so you can point them out kindly once (they are good revision items themselves).

## Reading

### I. Læseforståelse 1 — 25 minutes, 15 questions
1. Know vocabulary like: *eller/ellers* (other), *næsten ikke/næppe* (hardly), the difference between modal verbs
   *kunne/skulle/måtte/ville*, all question words *hvem/hvor/hvorfor/hvornår/hvordan*.
2. Know useful verbs: *at betyde, at anbefale, at fremhæve, at vare, at ansøge, at bestille, at optage,
   at undlade, at udelukke, at omfatte, at uddele, at fordele*.
3. Use the titles/section names to search for the answer and save time.
4. Read for **3 minutes** shortly/scan the reading sheet before trying to find answers, to get an impression of what the brochure is about.
5. Go to the assignment paper only after scanning the structure and each page of the reading paper.
6. Answer **shortly and precisely** — not whole sentences; often one word is enough.
7. Spelling must be as correct as possible.
8. **Never stick** to a challenging question. Can't find it → next one. Others may be easier.
9. **Remember:** write your answers on the right line.

Before the exam: read informative texts (brochures, leaflets, pamphlets) regularly; practise past papers (2012 onwards); get used to the question types.

### II. Læseforståelse 2 — 65 minutes, 2A/2B + 3
**2A/2B**
1. Learn the list of logical connectors to understand logical relations (see `connectors.md`; the plan includes
   the Konsekvens / Sammenligning / Modsætning table).
2. Know vocabulary like: *udsagn, i overensstemmelse, at give udtryk for, at afgøre, afgørende, en årsag,
   et vilkår, et forhold, i forhold til, om/hvorvidt*.
3. 2A multiple choice: **read the text first, then the questions** — otherwise you're biased while reading.
4. Find answers as close to the text as possible; don't add meaning or draw conclusions based on the answer options. **Stick to the information in the text.**
5. Draw conclusions only on what's in the text, not on the options — the wrong ones contain a confusing element.
6. Avoid generalising/specifying when the question doesn't require it.
7. Spot the confusing elements in the answer options.

**3 (gap fill)**
1. While solving previous years, make a list of useful, frequent verbs/adjectives/nouns **with their prepositions**:
   *et formål med, problemer med, effekten af, indflydelse på*.
2. Divide words into logical/synonym groups:
   - *at forhindre, at stoppe, at begrænse, at forebygge, at undgå*
   - *at betinge, at sikre, at garantere, at medføre, at føre til, at resultere i*
   - *at forklare, at udelukke, at begrænse, at understrege, at inkludere, at kræve*
3. Spot options that are synonyms or from the same logical group (e.g. *derfor & således* — both consequence;
   *godkende & acceptere* — both "accept"). **If two options mean the same, both are wrong.**
4. Annotated example (a past L3 grid): *medfører = fører til noget*; *forhindrer* (en forhindring = en barriere);
   *at regne → udregningen af* (kalkulation); *tillade ≠ forbyde*; *udelukke ≈ ekskludere*; *afvise = reject*;
   *nemlig ≈ "det er fordi"*; *næppe = hardly* ("Jeg nåede det næppe" / *med nød og næppe*).

## Writing — exercise plan

### I. Email (Delprøve 1)
- Opening: *Hej [navn],* / *Det er altid rart at høre fra dig.*
- Q1 frames: *Det vil være …* / *Jeg mener, at …*
- Q2 frames: *Det kommer an på, om …* / *Hvis man ser på det fra et andet perspektiv, …*
- Q3 frames: *Det kan få konsekvenser for …* / *Det betyder dog ikke nødvendigvis, at …*
- Closing: *Tusind tak for din besked, og jeg håber, vi kan snakke mere om det snart!* / *Med venlig hilsen / Din ven*

### II. Delprøve 2B — minimum 200 words (aim 250–300)
Order of work: **question 3 first**, then 1, then 2.

1. **Question 3 — fordele og ulemper (≈50%, 125–150 words)**
   - Start with a generic sentence: *Der er mange fordele og ulemper ved …*
   - For (vurdere): dit synspunkt → dit argument → uddyb med 1–2 eksempler
   - Et modstridende synspunkt → et kort argument → uddyb med ét kort eksempel
   - Din nuancerede vurdering — tie it together: "despite both sides of the argument, this is what I think…"
   - **Always end with a conclusion and a clear decision, for or against**:
     *For at konkludere: Jeg synes, at …* / *Så der er både fordele og ulemper, men fordelene vejer tungest.*
2. **Question 1 (15–20%, 30–50 words)**: always an intro sentence; relate to the points from question 3; no repetition.
3. **Question 2 (30–35%, 70–100 words)**: choose 2 points — one important, one less important. Use connectors:
   *alligevel* (nevertheless), *desuden/derudover*† (moreover), *imidlertid* (however), *selvom* (even though), *fordi* (because).

   Worked example — "Er det en god idé at forbyde dieselbiler i større byer?":
   > At forbyde dieselbiler i større byer kan virke som en bæredygtig mulighed, imidlertid er det tvivlsomt†, om det vil have
   > en hensigtsmæssig effekt. F.eks. er det ikke alle, der vil have økonomisk … Desuden er det ikke sikkert, at det vil reducere
   > luftforureningen, da der ikke er mange dieselbiler alligevel. Omvendt kan det give fordele til …

   **Template**: [Sætning] *kan virke som* [positiv beskrivelse], *imidlertid er det* [tvivlsomt/problematisk], *om/at* [hovedproblem].
   *F.eks.* [konkret eksempel — hvem det rammer], *fordi* [årsag]. *Desuden* [andet argument]. *Omvendt kan det være en fordel for en
   gruppe, fordi* [årsag]. *Alt i alt* [kort holdning].

   | Step | Function | Connector | Example |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1. Opening | Restate the exact sentence | *kan virke som …, imidlertid …* | *Kan virke som en bæredygtig mulighed, imidlertid …* |
   | 2. Example | A concrete case | *F.eks.* | *F.eks. er det ikke alle, der …* |
   | 3. Extra reason | A second argument | *Desuden / Derudover* | *Desuden er det ikke sikkert, at …* |
   | 4. Counterpoint | The other side | *Omvendt* | *Omvendt kan det give fordele i forhold til …* |

4. **Insert golden nuggets and fancy sentences** (see `connectors.md` → phrase bank).
5. **Revise what you wrote**
   - All questions answered? (*fordi* — did you give reasons?)
   - Read it and check complicated sentences.
   - Incorrect-word traps: *tror / synes / mener*; *ved / kender*; *da / når*.
   - Verbs + adjectives: tense, gender (en/et agreement), two verbs together (modal + infinitive), two nouns together → compound, conjugations.
   - Spelling mistakes.
   - Punctuation: comma before *men*; *der/som* relative clauses.
   - Too many corrections in one sentence → rewrite the sentence.

## Vocabulary sheets (to be filled during practice)
- **Reasoning/consequence**: *derfor* (therefore), *desuden* (furthermore), *ligeledes* (in the same way), *blandt andet* (among other things), *fordi* (because), *følgelig* (consequently)
- **Change of direction/contrast**: *alligevel* (in spite of this), *derimod*† (as opposed to / on the other hand), *selvom* (even though)
- **Adjectives**: *overraskende* (surprising), *logisk* (logical), *forventeligt*† (expectable), *nødvendigt*† (necessary)

The sheets were left mostly empty on purpose — fill them from the vocab deck (`pd3.py vocab list --group …`).

† Corrections of the original: *Derud over* → **derudover**; *trivlsomt* → **tvivlsomt**; *Derimond* → **derimod**;
*forventligt* → **forventeligt**; *nødventligt* → **nødvendigt**.
